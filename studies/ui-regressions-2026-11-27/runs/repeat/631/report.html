manual0.000000 Type or replace the input text, leave the field, and inspect the parsed value.
range0.000000 Select dates in a range or multiple-date mode and check its endpoints and disabled dates.
time0.000000 Change a time field, trigger a time update and inspect the selected hours, minutes and time limits.
